What can slow a custom song down

  • Missing details. Names, spellings and the story are the raw material. The more you share up front, the less back-and-forth there is.
  • Changes after delivery. If you want something changed, it takes a little more time, so leave room before a fixed date.
  • Payment that does not match your order. Use the same email address at checkout that you used on the order form. That is how your payment is matched to your story.
  • Extras that are separate steps. At some companies, putting your song on streaming services takes several extra days. For CustomSongMe get well song cards, the song link and printable card arrive within 24 hours, and a mailed card is sent within 3 business days after you approve it.
