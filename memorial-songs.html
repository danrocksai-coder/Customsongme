What is a memorial or celebration of life song?

A memorial song, also called a celebration of life song, is an original song written about someone who has passed, built from their story, their personality and the moments that meant the most. At CustomSongMe, it's created with AI-assisted songwriting and music tools from what you share and personally reviewed by Dan the Song Man with care, delivered within 24 hours of confirmed payment. It costs $25 for the MP3 only, $50 for the Music Video Release (adds a music video on YouTube), $75 for the Keepsake Set or $100 for the Collector's Edition, and keeping it private (a Google Drive instead of a public post) is +$25 on Packages 2, 3 and 4.
