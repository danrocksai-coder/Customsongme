How Much Does a Custom Song Cost?

By Dan Rock, Dan the Song Man · Updated October 2026

A custom song can cost anywhere from under $20 to more than $1,000. The price depends mostly on how much human time goes into it, how fast you need it, and what you get with it. Here is what each level looks like, so you can pick what fits.

Why the prices are so different

  • How much human time goes in. A service that pairs you with a working artist for several days costs far more than a tool that finishes in minutes.
  • How fast you get it. Rush delivery usually costs extra. A last-minute gift is where speed matters most.
  • What comes with it. A bare audio file costs less than a package with a video, cover art and lyrics.
  • Revisions and guarantees. Some companies allow a limited number of changes, and some say they do not offer refunds. Others offer a guarantee.

What to check before you buy from anyone

  • Will the lyrics use your real names and details, or just swap a name into a template?
  • How long does delivery take, and is there a rush fee?
