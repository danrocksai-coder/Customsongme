# Data Retention and Deletion Policy: Family Memory Magnets

Version 1, November 17, 2026. Matches the public privacy policy.

| Data | Kept until | Then |
|---|---|---|
| Story, recordings, hellos, family photos, profile answers | No deadline while recording. After the last conversation: 12 months. Unfinished with no activity for 2 years: contact the family, then delete | Deleted with Admin > Finish & delete |
| "Write It First" notes | Until that recording is saved | Deleted automatically |
| Uploaded consent form | Same as the story | Deleted with the story |
| Consent record (who / when / version) | As long as needed to show compliance (suggest 3 years) | Deleted |
| Orders / waiting list entries | As long as needed for taxes and support | Deleted on request where the law allows |

## Routine
- **Monthly:** open Admin. Any story with a red "keep-until date has passed" banner: deliver anything owed, then Finish & delete.
- **On a deletion request:** Admin shows a red banner. Deliver anything the family asks for, then delete within 30 days, and confirm to the family.
- **Never** keep children's recordings longer than needed for the keepsake.
