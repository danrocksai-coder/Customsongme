## How the product limits legal risk
1. **Parent-run by default.** The family link belongs to the parent, who records together with the child.
2. **Child's own link is opt-in, by the parent only.** 13+: the parent confirms age. Under 13: the parent uploads a signed consent form, Dan approves it, then the parent turns the link on. The buyer and Dan can never turn it on.
3. **Parent can turn it off, withdraw consent, listen to everything, and ask for deletion at any time.**
4. **Minimal data:** child's first name, voice and chosen/typed questions only.
5. **No ads, no selling, no AI training, no voice cloning or voiceprints.**
6. **Publishing needs a separate yes** from the parent and the grandparent.
