Where UniqueSong has the edge

  • The free preview. You hear part of your song before you pay. If that matters to you, it is a real advantage. I do not offer it.
  • Size and reviews. They say they have made more than 10,000 songs and show thousands of reviews. I am one person with a smaller track record.
  • Very fast rush. Their refund policy mentions a 30-minute rush song. My fastest option is 4 studio hours.

Where CustomSongMe has the edge

  • You see the price first. All four packages and every add-on are listed on the order page before you start.
  • Faster standard delivery. Every order arrives within 24 hours of confirmed payment, and Rush is +$10.
  • A guarantee with no short clock. If the song is not right, I work with you to make it right. If I still cannot, you get a full refund.
  • You deal with one person. I read your story and review every song myself, and you can text me any time.
  • Something to hold. Every package includes a printable keepsake card with your lyrics and a QR code.

Things to know about CustomSongMe

  • Songs are created with AI-assisted songwriting and music tools, and every one is personally reviewed by Dan the Song Man. Full details are on the Privacy and Terms page.
  • Packages 2 to 4 include a YouTube video that is posted publicly on my channel. That listing is part of how I keep prices low. Add Keep it Private for +$25 and I post nothing.
  • It is a one-time payment, with no subscriptions.

Which one should you choose?

  • You want to hear your song before paying: UniqueSong.
  • You want a lot of reviews to look through: UniqueSong.
  • You want to know the price up front and get the song within a day: CustomSongMe.
  • You want to talk to the person reviewing your song, and a guarantee that is not limited to the first few hours: CustomSongMe.
