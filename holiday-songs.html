Christmas Song Ideas for Everyone on Your List

The best Christmas songs sound like they could only be about one family. Here's what I like to hear about, depending on who it's for:

  • For mom or dad: the traditions they started, the recipe that has to be on the table and the thing they say every single Christmas.
  • For a wife, husband or partner: your first Christmas together, the year everything changed, or the gift you still laugh about.
  • For kids: a song from Santa that names each kid, what they did this year and what's on their list. It's a sing-along they'll ask for every December.
  • For grandma or grandpa: the family they built, with every grandkid's name in the chorus. It's an easy gift to play on a video call if you can't be there.
  • For the whole family: a year-in-review carol with the trips, the new baby, the new house and the funny moments.
  • For coworkers or the team: lighthearted and office-friendly, made for the holiday party.
  • For someone you're missing: a gentle song about a loved one who isn't at the table this year. See also our celebration of life songs.
