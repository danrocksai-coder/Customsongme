How it works

  1. Tell me about the two of you. Answer at least 4 of 7 short questions (300 characters each).
  2. Choose a style. Sonnet, rhyming poem, free verse or short & sweet; modern or classic language.
  3. Pick your favorite of four poems. Edit any word yourself, or ask for up to two AI revisions.
  4. Choose a design and add a photo if you like. Preview the finished page with a watermark.
  5. Pay securely and download your print-ready PDF right away. With the voice package, record yourself and the QR code plays it.

Questions

Who writes the poems?

My AI poem writer drafts four poems from the answers you give. You choose your favorite, edit any word you like, and approve the final version. Nothing is delivered until you approve it.

How do I get my poem?

Right after payment you can download a print-ready PDF (8.5 x 11) with no watermark. It is also emailed to you with a private link to download it again.

How does the voice recording work?

With the $25 package you record yourself reading the poem right on this page, using your phone or computer microphone. A QR code printed on the page plays your recording. You can re-record any time and the same QR code plays the new version. It is always your real voice, never an AI voice.

Is tax included?

Prices are $20 and $25 plus applicable tax. Any tax is calculated by Stripe and shown before you pay.

Is my story private?

Yes. Your answers, photo and recording are only used to make your poem. Poems are never posted publicly. Unfinished drafts are deleted after 14 days.
