Parent or Guardian Consent

Family Memory Magnets · Child's own recording link (children under 13) · Form version 2026-11-17

Family Memory Magnets helps a grandchild and a grandparent record 30 short voice conversations, which become a private family keepsake. By default, a parent records together with the child on the parent's phone. This form lets a parent allow their child to have their own recording link instead.

What is collected from the child: the child's first name, the child's voice recordings (questions up to 15 seconds and optional voice hellos up to 15 seconds), and the questions the child picks or types.

How it is used: only to run the family's story and to make the family's keepsake (movie, storybook and recordings). It is never sold, never used for advertising, and never used to train AI or to create a voice copy.

Who can hear it: the parent, the grandparent in this story, and Family Memory Magnets staff who make the keepsake. Recordings are stored privately with our hosting provider. Nothing is posted publicly, including on YouTube or the magnet's web page, unless the parent gives a separate yes later.

How long it is kept: while the story is being recorded (there is no deadline), then for 12 months after the last conversation is finished, and then deleted. If an unfinished story has no activity for 2 years, we contact the parent before deleting it. The parent can ask for deletion sooner.

Parent rights: at any time, the parent can listen to everything the child recorded, turn off the child's link, withdraw this consent, and ask for the child's recordings to be deleted. Full details are in the privacy policy at customsongme.com/family-memory-magnets/privacy/.
