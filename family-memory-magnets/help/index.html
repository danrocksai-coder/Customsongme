How it works for you

  1. Your grandchild asks a question. You get a message, or you simply scan your fridge magnet.
  2. You listen to their real voice asking you.
  3. You answer in your own words, for up to one minute. That's it.

There is no deadline. Answer whenever it suits you.

How to open your story

  1. Scan your magnet: open your phone's camera, point it at the square code on your magnet, and tap the link that pops up.
  2. Or tap your private link in the text or email you were sent.
  3. The first time, open your private link once. After that, your magnet opens your story on that phone every time.

Tip: keep it one tap away. On an iPhone, tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen. On Android, tap the three dots, then Add to Home screen.

How to record your answer

  1. Tap Listen and Record My Answer.
  2. Tap the play button to hear the question. Listen as many times as you like.
  3. Tap the big Tap to record button and talk. A timer counts down from one minute, and it warns you 5 seconds before the end.
  4. Tap Tap to stop when you're done, or it stops by itself.
  5. Listen back. Happy? Tap Send this recording. Not happy? Tap Record again.
  6. Wait for "Saved. Your recording is safe." Then you're done.

Like to think first?

Tap Write It First to jot down a few notes. They appear in big letters while you record, like a teleprompter. Only you can see them.

Rather not answer a question? Tap Skip this question, and your grandchild will pick a different one. That's completely fine.

The microphone isn't working

Your phone needs to allow the microphone for this page. Here's how:

iPhone (Safari)
  1. Tap the aA button next to the web address.
  2. Tap Website Settings.
  3. Set Microphone to Allow.
  4. Go back and tap record again.
Android (Chrome)
  1. Tap the small icon left of the web address.
  2. Tap Permissions.
  3. Turn Microphone on.
  4. Go back and tap record again.

Still no luck? Open your link in Safari on an iPhone, or Chrome on Android, rather than inside a texting or email app.

"It didn't send"

Don't worry. Your recording stays safely on your phone until it is saved. When your internet is back, tap Try sending again. You'll only see "Saved" once it has really arrived.

New phone or lost link?

Just scan your magnet on the new phone. If you set a PIN, type it in. If anything is stuck, text Dan and he'll send you a fresh link right away.

Your profile

You'll see 20 quick tap-to-answer questions about you: where you grew up, your first job, your favorite music. Every one is optional, and there's no rush. They help finish your family movie and storybook.

Little Hellos

After a quiet couple of days, you may see a chance to send a short "thinking of you" note or a 15-second voice hello to your grandchild. It's just for fun, and it never changes your story.

Your recordings are private

  • Only your family and Dan can hear them.
  • Voice only. There is never any video.
  • Nothing is shared publicly unless you and the parent both say yes.
