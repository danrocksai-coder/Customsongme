FAMILY MEMORY MAGNETS

Privacy Policy

Effective November 17, 2026

Family Memory Magnets helps a grandchild and a grandparent record 30 short voice conversations that become a private family keepsake. This policy explains what we collect, why, who can see it, how long we keep it, and the choices you have. It covers the Family Memory Magnets pages and app at customsongme.com/family-memory-magnets/.

Children's privacy

We take children's privacy seriously and follow the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

  • Parent-run by default. A parent holds the family recording link and records together with the child on the parent's phone.
  • Permission first. Nothing a child says is saved until a parent or legal guardian gives permission.
  • Child's own link (optional). A parent may choose to give the child their own recording link. For a child under 13, we require a signed parent consent form, which we review before the link can be turned on. For a child 13 or older, the parent confirms the child's age. Only the parent can turn the link on, and the parent can turn it off at any time. The person who bought the gift cannot make this choice for the parent.
  • What we collect from or about the child: first name, voice recordings (questions up to 15 seconds and optional voice hellos up to 15 seconds), and the questions the child picks or types. We do not ask a child for an email address, phone number, last name, address, photo or birthday.
  • We do not sell children's information, show ads, track children across other websites, use recordings to train AI, or create voice copies or voiceprints.
  • We collect only what the story needs, and a child does not have to share more to take part.

What we collect

  • From the parent or buyer: name, email, optional phone, the names used in the story (for example "Mimi"), consent records (who agreed, when, and to which version), and, if used, a photo or PDF of the signed consent form. Payments are handled by Stripe; we never see or store card numbers.
  • From the grandparent: voice recordings (answers up to 60 seconds, optional introduction and closing messages up to 2 minutes), optional voice hellos, and optional private "Write It First" notes, which only the writer can see and which are deleted once the recording is saved.
  • From the child: as described above.
  • Technical information: a random sign-in code stored on each phone so the link remembers it, and basic error reports when an upload fails. We do not use advertising or analytics cookies on these pages.

How we use it

Only to run your family's story, to make your keepsake (family movie, storybook and recordings), to help you when something goes wrong, and to keep records of consent. Nothing else.

Who can see or hear it

  • The people in your story: the parent, the grandparent, and the child (if they have their own link).
  • Dan Rock, to make your keepsake and to help with problems. Admin access is password-protected.
  • Service providers that store or deliver data for us and may use it only for that purpose: Netlify (website hosting and private storage of recordings), Stripe (payments), and Formspree (sends Dan notices about orders and forms; it never receives recordings). If we add another provider, such as a transcription service for the storybook, we will update this policy first.
  • Publishing is a separate yes. Your finished movie goes on YouTube (as an unlisted video) or on the magnet's web page only if a parent and the grandparent each give a separate yes. Unlisted means anyone with the link can watch it, so we will explain this again before asking.
  • We may disclose information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • A story is open for 12 months from the day it is created. When the 30th conversation is finished, it stays available until 12 months after that day.
  • After that, recordings, notes, consent forms and sign-in codes are deleted. We keep a short record of the consent (who agreed and when) only as long as needed to show we followed the law.
  • You can ask for deletion sooner at any time from your parent page or by contacting us. We delete within 30 days, after delivering anything you ask us to send you first.

Your choices and rights

A parent or guardian can, at any time:

  • listen to everything their child has recorded;
  • pause recording, turn off the child's own link, or withdraw consent;
  • ask us to delete their child's information;
  • refuse further collection. Withdrawing consent stops new recordings by the child; recordings already saved stay in your story until you ask for deletion.

Grandparents and parents can also ask to see or delete their own recordings. Contact us using the details above.

How we protect it

  • Recordings are stored privately and are never at a public web address. They play only through links that expire after 5 minutes.
  • Private links and PINs are stored only in scrambled (hashed) form, can be cancelled instantly, and lock out after repeated wrong PINs.
  • The magnet's QR code is not a password. On its own it shows only "A Family Memory Magnets story is being created."
  • We follow a written security plan and review it at least once a year.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we collect or use children's information, we will tell parents and ask for new consent before the change applies to their child.
