Questions and answers

What is Family Memory Magnets?

A gift where a grandchild asks a grandparent short questions in their own voice, and the grandparent answers in theirs. After 30 or 60 conversations, the recordings become a family movie, a digital storybook and a set of recordings to keep, opened by a QR fridge magnet in each home.

What is a meaningful gift for grandparents who have everything?

Time and attention from their grandchild. Instead of another object, Family Memory Magnets gives a grandparent a reason to tell their life story to the grandchild who asks, and gives the family a movie of their voices to keep.

How does a grandchild interview a grandparent?

The child picks one of three suggested questions at each stop (fun, curious or meaningful), or writes their own, and records it in up to 15 seconds. The grandparent gets a private link, listens to the real question, and records an answer of up to 60 seconds.

What questions do grandkids ask?

The questions follow six chapters of a life: where it all began, growing up, the road to adulthood, love and family, life's mountains, and the legacy garden. For example: what their house looked like as a kid, who their best friend was, how they met, and what they want the family to remember.

Is it a good gift for long-distance grandparents?

Yes. Each person records on their own phone, whenever it suits them, so a grandparent across the country can stay close to a grandchild one question at a time.

How is it different from Storyworth, Remento or a memory book?

Those services send a company's questions to the grandparent, who writes or records the answers for a book. Here the grandchild does the asking, in their own voice, so both voices are saved, and the result is a family movie and a fridge magnet as well as a storybook.

Should we choose 30 or 60 conversations?

30 conversations make a full family story and are designed to take about 6 months. The Big Story doubles it to 60, about a year. With Grandma and Grandpa together, they take turns, so each answers half.

Is there a deadline?

No. The pace is a suggestion. Your story stays open until it is finished.

Does the grandparent need an app or a password?

No. They open a private link on their phone or scan their magnet, tap play to hear the question, and tap record. They can listen back, record again, or skip any question.

Is it safe for children?

Yes. It is voice only, never video. A parent always gives permission first and controls the settings, and children never upload photos. Recordings stay private, and the movie is shared only if the parent and the grandparent both say yes.

Who controls the settings?

The person who buys it. They can fill in each grandparent's profile, add photos, pause recording, and decide whether the child records with a parent or, if mature enough, on their own by scanning their QR code.

What do we get when the story is finished?

A family movie with your photos and both voices, a digital storybook, every recording to download, and two QR fridge magnets that open it all.

When do the magnets arrive?

Your two custom magnets are ordered within 24 hours, and I email you when they will arrive. Meanwhile you get two personal QR codes by email to print, so you can start right away.

What ages is it for?

Any child who can ask a question. Younger children record with a parent beside them; the parent decides when a child is ready to record on their own.
