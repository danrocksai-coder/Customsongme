How it works

A grandchild asks the questions. A grandparent answers in their own voice.

Choose your story. Create a story with Grandma, Grandpa, or a package celebrating both. Each grandparent gets a personal journey of 30 meaningful conversations.
Order and get your link. After ordering, you receive a confirmation and a link to begin. A parent helps set things up, and your personalized QR magnet is prepared for delivery.
Personalize it. Enter the child's name and the grandparent's name. For example, a little girl named Devon creating a story with her grandmother, Mimi.
Open your family story. Explore six chapters with five special stops in each, and watch your collection of memories grow.
The child picks a question. At every stop the child chooses a suggested question, or writes their own, then taps record and asks it out loud (up to 30 seconds).
The grandparent answers. A private invitation goes to the grandparent. They listen to the real question, then record their memories in their own words and voice (up to 60 seconds). No complicated technology. They can listen back, record again, then submit.
The adventure continues. The child listens to the answer and discovers things about their grandparent they may never have known. Then the next question unlocks.
No rush. Childhood memories, family traditions, adventures and lessons learned, at your own pace, throughout your 12-month adventure.
A family history of your own. When all 30 conversations are complete, the journey becomes a family movie that brings the memories to life with photographs and your original voices.
A keepsake that lasts. You also receive a personalized refrigerator magnet with a QR code that opens the movie, plus a digital storybook and downloadable recordings, so your family can preserve and share it all.

Private by design

  • Each person gets a private link. No accounts and no passwords.
  • A parent or guardian gives permission before a child's recording is saved.
  • Recordings are never public. The QR magnet's public page shows no names, photos or recordings.
  • You can ask for your family's recordings to be deleted.

Questions

Does the grandparent need an app?
No. They open a private link on their phone, listen, and tap record.
How long do we have?
Your story stays open for 12 months from the day it is created. There is no rush within that time.
What if a grandparent doesn't want to answer a question?
They can skip it, and the child picks a different one.
What do we get when it's finished?
A family movie with photographs and your original voices, a personalized refrigerator magnet with a QR code that opens the movie, a digital storybook, and downloadable recordings.
