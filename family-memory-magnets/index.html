How it works

Order. Within a day you get private links for the parent and the grandparent. No apps, no passwords.
Talk, one question at a time. The child picks a question and records it with a parent. The grandparent listens and answers in their own voice. It's designed to take about 6 months (about a year for the Big Story), with no deadline.
Keep it forever. When you finish, you get your family movie, storybook and every recording. Scan the magnet to watch.

Private by design

  • The parent always gives permission first, and controls the settings.
  • Recordings and photos stay private. The movie is shared on the magnet or YouTube only if the parent and grandparent both say yes.
  • You can ask for everything to be deleted at any time.

Questions

What will they talk about?
Six chapters, from childhood to legacy: where it all began, growing up, finding their way, love and family, life's journey, and what matters most. At each stop the child picks a fun, curious or meaningful question, or writes their own.
30 or 60 conversations?
30 is a full family story. The Big Story doubles it to 60, for twice the memories. With Grandma and Grandpa together, they take turns, so each answers half.
What is the profile?
Each grandparent gets 20 quick multiple-choice questions (where they grew up, their first job, their favorite music…). It takes a few minutes, every answer is optional, and it helps finish the movie and storybook.
Does the grandparent need an app?
No. They open a private link on their phone, listen, and tap record. They can skip any question.
How long does it take?
30 conversations are designed to take about 6 months, and the Big Story (60) about a year. There is no deadline: your story stays open until you finish it.
