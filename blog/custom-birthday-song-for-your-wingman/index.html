Why a song beats a card

A card gets read once and tossed in a drawer. A song gets played at the party, sent to the group chat, and replayed on a bad day. Because it's built from real details, it feels like you really know the person. That's the whole idea behind a custom birthday song: original music about one person, using their name, their stories and their inside jokes.

What goes into a good wingman song

The more real detail you share, the better the song. Think about:

  • The nickname only your group uses.
  • The story everyone retells: the road trip, the wrong turn, the night nothing went to plan.
  • The vibe: sentimental, funny, or both. Pick a genre they'd actually listen to.
  • What you want them to know, even if you'd never say it out loud.
